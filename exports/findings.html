Toronto café review study findings


Status: **complete**


Scope and limits


  • Toronto municipal study envelope only; surrounding municipalities excluded.
  • Target 12 stores/brand; achieved 33 stores.
  • Up to 30 newest reviews per store within 12 calendar months ending 2026-11-18T00:59:19.806029-05:00.
  • Reviewers are self-selected; store selection is purposive; newest-first caps cover different spans.
  • This study does not establish demand for a bar or predict financial returns.
  • Do not treat results as representative of all Toronto cafés or national brand performance.

    • Geography sources


  • Operational City of Toronto study envelope approximated from public municipal extents (Lake Ontario south, Steeles Ave north, Rouge/Pickering boundary east, Etobicoke Creek west).
  • Downtown operational polygon for this study: roughly Bathurst–Don River–Bloor–waterfront core.
  • Campus points from publicly listed campus coordinates; university-adjacent = within 800 m.
  • Suburban neighbourhood centres are this study's operational definition, not official planning boundaries.

    • Findings


    1. Recent ratings among sampled stores


    Among the sampled stores, equal-store average recent ratings were Tim Hortons 4.12 (n_stores=11, n_reviews=305), Second Cup 3.44 (n_stores=8, n_reviews=146), Starbucks 3.21 (n_stores=11, n_reviews=249). These are newest-first capped samples, not complete annual histories.


    2. Topic complaints: atmosphere_seating


    Among eligible stores (≥10 analyzed text reviews), complaint prevalence for atmosphere_seating was Starbucks 9% (eligible stores=8), Second Cup 2% (eligible stores=5), Tim Hortons 2% (eligible stores=9). Mixed sentiment counts toward complaints and praise.


    3. Topic complaints: cleanliness


    Among eligible stores (≥10 analyzed text reviews), complaint prevalence for cleanliness was Tim Hortons 4% (eligible stores=9), Second Cup 3% (eligible stores=5), Starbucks 1% (eligible stores=8). Mixed sentiment counts toward complaints and praise.


    4. Area patterns are descriptive


    Downtown/suburban/other differences use this study's operational geography and small purposive samples. University-adjacent stores overlap those area groups and cannot be summed as exclusive categories. Second Cup downtown mean=3.49 (n=7); Second Cup suburban mean=3.10 (n=1); Starbucks downtown mean=3.20 (n=10); Starbucks suburban mean=3.37 (n=1); Tim Hortons downtown mean=4.20 (n=9); Tim Hortons other mean=3.70 (n=1); Tim Hortons suburban mean=3.83 (n=1)


    5. Praise themes also appear


    Highest observed praise prevalence in the eligible-store summary was Tim Hortons on service at 62% (eligible stores=9). This does not imply overall superiority.

    • Methods notes


  • Recent store rating = mean of collected ratings including textless ratings.
  • Topic prevalences exclude textless ratings and failed analysis; failed ≠ neutral.
  • Brand topic metrics average eligible store prevalences (equal store weight).
  • Lifetime Google ratings are reported separately from recent collected means.
